## Post Content

$24,500/month retainer vs. an 8-minute pipeline run.

Last month, a Series B SaaS founder ran an experiment on their Q2 product launch.

Brief A went to their incumbent London performance agency: 14 people on the Slack channel, account directors, and weekly syncs. 

Brief B went to a 2-person autonomous agency running Claude, Cursor, and Make.

Same positioning document. Same ad spend ($15,000).

The legacy agency delivered 6 ad concepts in 19 business days. Three were variations of the same safe Figma carousel, billed at $175/hour for creative strategy.

The 2-person shop delivered 48 localized video variants, 12 landing page angles, and full attribution webhooks in 38 hours.

Cost for Agency A: $24,500 retainer + two weeks of bottleneck calls.
Cost for Agency B: $4,200 project fee + $184 in API tokens.

Traditional agencies sell headcount to justify hours. AI agencies sell working systems that compound.

When your billable model rewards friction, speed looks like a threat to margins.

If you run an agency today, what is the single operational task your clients still pay you to do manually that an autonomous stack could run before breakfast?

P.S. If you're building an autonomous agency stack, share whether you're pricing on compute tokens or performance outcomes.
